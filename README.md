## Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI business intelligence solution designed to analyze mobile phone sales performance across brands, models, cities, payment methods, customer ratings and time periods.

The objective of the project was to transform transactional mobile sales data into actionable business insights that can support sales planning, product strategy, customer experience improvement and revenue optimization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and transaction performance
- Analyze monthly sales and quantity trends
- Identify top-performing brands and mobile models
- Understand customer satisfaction levels
- Analyze payment method preferences
- Identify geographic sales patterns
- Evaluate day-wise sales performance
- Support inventory and promotional decisions

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Revenue Performance
The business generated approximately ₹769.2M in total sales with an average order value of approximately ₹200.57K.

### 2. Monthly Trend
July recorded the highest monthly quantity at approximately 1,700 units, while November recorded the lowest at approximately 1,038 units.

This represents a significant decline in sales volume toward the end of the year and requires further investigation into seasonality, pricing, inventory and promotional activities.

### 3. Brand Performance
Apple generated the highest sales at approximately ₹161.6M, followed closely by Samsung at approximately ₹160.0M.

The relatively small difference between brands indicates a well-diversified product portfolio.

### 4. Product Performance
The iPhone SE was the strongest-performing displayed model with approximately ₹60M in sales.

### 5. Customer Satisfaction
Approximately 61% of customers/transactions were classified as Good, while around 39% were Average or Poor.

This represents a significant opportunity to improve customer experience.

### 6. Payment Behavior
UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card and Cash each contributed approximately 24–26% of transactions, indicating highly diversified payment preferences.

### 7. Geographic Performance
Sales are distributed across multiple Indian cities, with Delhi appearing among the stronger markets based on the geographic visualization.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the significant decline in sales volume during October–December.
2. Optimize inventory for high-performing mobile models.
3. Analyze Poor and Average ratings to identify customer experience issues.
4. Introduce payment-specific cashback and EMI campaigns.
5. Develop city-level sales strategies based on revenue and AOV.
6. Use brand and model-level profitability analysis to optimize product mix.
7. Implement promotional campaigns around high-performing sales days.
